    The Example

    You receive scores in the same way, regardless of the course model.

    Sorted by Assessment Type

    Not all applications of a skill demonstrate the student's ability as well as another, so scores need to be categorised and given different power over the final grade.

    In the Assessment Type Model, scores are categorised by the time and place (the assessment) where the HC or LO was applied.

    In-Class and Assignments

    The assessment categories are organised into either in-class scores or assignment scores, but this categorisation does not affect how much they are worth. They are only grouped this way for #Organisation.

    Each assignment and in-class assessment type is responsible for a share of the final grade.

    The Grade as an Area

    If your final grade is represented by this box, each assessment type has been given an area within it that is definite and fixed.

    Assignments Subdivided

    When you have not yet finished the course, the areas that have been assigned to future assessments are completely ‘cut away’. Your overall grade to date would be represented by the sub-area of whatever you have completed.

    Filled with Scores

    Each area gets filled by its scores, which means more scores (tagging more LOs) will ‘dilute’ the impact of each score.

    The Example

    You receive scores in the same way, regardless of the course model.

    Scores Are Copied

    Assignment scores are multiplied according to the associated assignment weight. An application of an LO in a 1× class poll will have half as much impact as an application in a 2× assignment.

    Sorted by Learning Outcome

    Not all applications of a skill demonstrate the student's ability as well as another, so scores need to be categorised and given different power over the final grade.

    In the CO Based Model, scores are categorised by LO and HC, but they are categorised again before the grouping is assigned its share of the final grade.

    LOs Form Course Outcomes

    The LOs (and sometimes HCs) all belong to a Course Outcome, which is the scale of analysis where score importance is dictated.

    Each CO is responsible for a share of the final grade.

    The Grade as Bins

    Each CO is a bin you will keep filling up all semester. If you have no scores in a CO, that bin is cut away completely from your grade to date.
